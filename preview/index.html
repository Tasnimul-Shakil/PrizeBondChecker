Prize Bond Rules & Guide
📅
Draw Schedule
Draws are conducted quarterly by Bangladesh Bank 4 times a year:
  • 31st January
  • 30th April
  • 31st July
  • 31st October
If any draw date falls on a weekend or public holiday, the draw takes place on the next working day.
⏳
2-Year Validity Window
You can claim prize money up to 2 years from the date of the draw. After 2 years, any unclaimed prize is forfeited to the Government treasury.
🎯
Series-Agnostic Matching
Bangladesh Bank draws are series-agnostic. Every existing series (e.g. কখ, গঘ, KA, etc.) having the drawn 7-digit serial number wins the designated prize!
🏆

Total: 46 prize numbers per draw.
🧾
Taxation & Claim Procedure
Under NBR regulations, 20% source tax is deducted from prize earnings. Submit claims at any Bangladesh Bank branch, National Savings Bureau, or scheduled bank with original bond, NID, and bank details.
